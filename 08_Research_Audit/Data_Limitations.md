# Data limitations

1. **No observed data analysed** (Dataverse unreachable from build environment). All results synthetic. CRITICAL for any empirical claim; the manuscript makes none.
2. Synthetic panel design is assumed (archetype shares, seasonal shape, noise) and only loosely informed by a published average (280 kWh/month May–Oct 2019). The synthetic May–Oct mean is 255.5 kWh.
3. Tariff scenario from a secondary trade-press source (Power Line, 12 Nov 2020); primary UPERC order not retrieved; telescopic application assumed; households' categories (urban/rural, lifeline, connected load) unknown even in the real data.
4. Rates changed during the dataset window (first urban slab Rs 4.90 → 5.50 in Sept 2019 per Power Line, 18 Sep 2019); a single schedule is applied as a counterfactual.
5. Sample of ~100 instrumented urban households is not representative of UP or India; no extrapolation is permissible.
6. Fixed consumption: no demand response; the smoothed comparator is hypothetical.
7. Billing cycles in practice follow meter-reading dates; calendar or 30-day cycles are approximations.
